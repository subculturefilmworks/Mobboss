How to open Mob Boss on Mac

1. Drag Mob Boss to Applications.
2. In Applications, right-click (Control-click) Mob Boss → Open → Open.
3. On macOS 15 or later, if blocked, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, then confirm Open.

Mob Boss is free software and is not paid-signed with Apple (the app uses an ad-hoc signature).
When macOS asks for drive or folder access, click Allow. It may ask again after an update.
